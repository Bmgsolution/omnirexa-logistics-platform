Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about shipping, tracking, payments, delivery and cargo security.

How do I track my shipment?

Enter your tracking number (e.g. GSL-2026-847291-XP) on the tracking portal or in the homepage hero widget to view live-style status, timeline and route.

Is the tracking data on this site real-time?

No. This is a prototype using demo/sample data and frontend animation to simulate what a real-time tracking experience would look like. It is not connected to a live GPS or carrier feed.

What shipping methods are available?

Standard Road, Express, Air Freight, Sea Freight, and Road Freight — each with different estimated transit times, shown on the shipping page.

How do I get a shipping quote?

Use the quote calculator on the shipping page or inside your customer dashboard. Estimates are generated from demo pricing logic.

How is my cargo kept secure?

OmniRexa's security services include GPS-style monitoring, tamper detection, secure warehousing, and driver verification — see the security page for details.

Can I choose delivery preferences like signature confirmation?

Yes — when creating a shipment in the customer dashboard, you can select delivery preferences such as signature required or photo confirmation.

What payment methods are supported?

This prototype does not process real payments. Invoices and payment records shown in the dashboards are sample data for demonstration.

What happens if my shipment is delayed?

Delayed shipments are flagged in the tracking timeline with a distinct status color, and (in production) would trigger email/SMS/push notifications.
